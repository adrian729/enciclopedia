# Fried Cinnamon Sugar Bananas

Back to [All Recipes](cooking/recipes/README.md)

> Total: 11 mins · Yield: 2 servings · Cost: under $1

## Ingredients

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 3 tablespoons | white sugar |
| 1½ teaspoons | cinnamon |
| 2 | bananas, sliced into quarters |
| 2 tablespoons | vegan butter |
| Scoop of | [Lickety-Split Ice Cream](cooking/recipes/ice-cream.md) (optional) |

## Preparation

1. In a medium bowl, mix together the sugar and cinnamon. Add the banana slices and gently toss to coat.
2. Melt the vegan butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once melted, add the bananas and fry for 2–3 minutes per side, until lightly golden and warmed through. Enjoy alone or serve with a scoop of vegan ice cream or other addition if desired.
