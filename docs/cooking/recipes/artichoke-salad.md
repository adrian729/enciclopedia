# Artichoke Salad

Back to [All Recipes](cooking/recipes/README.md)

> Total: 15 mins · Yield: 2 as a main, 4 as a side · Cost: under $10

## Ingredients

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 6 cups | baby spinach leaves |
| 1 can (19 ounces) | white beans, such as kidney or lima, drained and rinsed (about 2 cups) |
| 1 jar (6 ounces) | marinated artichoke hearts, chopped (use leftover liquid in [Artichoke Dressing](cooking/recipes/artichoke-dressing.md)) |
| 1 large | tomato, cut into wedges |
| ¼ | red onion, thinly sliced |
| 1 batch | [Artichoke Dressing](cooking/recipes/artichoke-dressing.md) |

## Preparation

1. Add the spinach, white beans, artichoke hearts, tomato wedges, and red onions to a large bowl. Drizzle the dressing overtop to taste, and lightly toss to coat.
